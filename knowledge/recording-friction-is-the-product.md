**The deck exists to make starting a snippet recording cost nothing. Every key
is judged by whether it lowers the friction of recording or raises the
production value of what gets recorded. A key that does neither goes.**

Ryan, 2026-09-02: *"The easier it is for me to just press a button and start
recording these snippets where I find an applicable point that I want to
present in video, I can spin it up right there. That ease of use and lack of
friction keeps me creating content."* And on the Status key: *"I don't need the
ready button because usually I'll just press the screen-and-me because I know
that actually works and it opens the OBS app."*

How to apply: the Status (READY) key is removed. Any scene key cold-starts OBS
already, so a dedicated launcher is a duplicate. New keys must name which of
the two jobs they do: less friction to record, or more production value in
the frame (camera shots, zoom, cutouts). Broadcast health stays parked.
